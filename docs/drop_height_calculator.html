Robustness Calculator

Calculate robustness as admissable impact impulse $p_\rm{max}$ or admissable drop height $h_\rm{max}$ for custom actuator and link parameters and impact parameters. Effective motor inertia $I_{\rm{a}}=N^2 I_m$ has a reciproacl influence on robustness. When normalized with normalzized impact $p_\rm{ref}$ and $I_l$ the log-log plot curvature is invariant of other parameters. The ratio of maximum curvature $\gamma$ follows for a worst case contact time $\Delta_t=0$.
